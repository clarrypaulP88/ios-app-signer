Quick check reads your Deco, this computer's Wi-Fi, ping and DNS (about 20 seconds). Full check also runs an internet speed test (about 20 seconds more). It uses a few hundred MB of data, up to about 1 GB on a very fast plan.

Connect to Deco

  • It needs the Deco owner password: the TP-Link ID password you use in the Deco app. Manager accounts can't log in here.
  • Connecting logs the owner out of the Deco app on their phone, because a Deco only allows one owner login at a time. Just log in on the app again afterwards (or press Disconnect first).
  • Your password is never saved. It stays in this computer's memory and is gone when you disconnect or stop the dashboard.

Settings

Your plan speed is on your internet bill or in your provider's app. We use it to check if you get what you pay for. Mbps means megabits per second.
